01 — Arrival

A world that breathes light.

Four billion years of weather, drawn in a single glance. Aether turns slowly, as if it knew you were coming.

02 — Atmosphere

Where day lets go of night.

At the terminator, cities wake in gold while storms rewrite the sky below. Every cloud here is computed, not painted.

03 — Rings

Ten thousand years, in motion.

Hundreds of thousands of fragments of ice and stone keep a quiet orbit. Slip between them.

04 — Debris

The ruins of a moon that never formed.

Each rock carries its own scars. Fly close enough to read them.

05 — Nebula

Light, before it learns a name.

A cloud of ionized gas, lit from within by stars not yet born.

06 — Core

Touch the heart of it.

Everything you passed was only its echo. Move your cursor. It answers.
